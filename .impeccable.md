## Design Context

### Users
The dashboard is used by IEEE at UC San Diego general officers and higher-access officers while managing club operations. They need to submit, review, and track operational requests quickly without losing confidence in the status or next action.

### Brand Personality
Polished, nice, neat. The interface should feel production-ready, official, organized, and calm.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing light dashboard system, IEEE blue brand tokens, restrained status colors, clear typography, and tidy spacing. The experience should be professional and unobtrusive, with no overwhelming visual treatments.

### Design Principles
- Keep screens quiet and scannable so officers can understand status and next actions quickly.
- Prefer existing tokens and shared UI components over one-off styling.
- Make interaction targets clear, accessible, and consistent across mouse, keyboard, and touch.
- Use status color sparingly and purposefully; avoid loud decorative elements.
- Polish to a ready-for-production bar: aligned, responsive, typed, and verified.
