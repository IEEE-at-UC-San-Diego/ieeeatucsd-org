Dashboard Merch Store

Durable points ledger, inventory-safe checkout, scheduled pickup, QR fulfillment, and administrative operations.

Design approved Scope: apps/dashboard Backend: Convex Delivery: 7 reviewable PRs Timezone: America/Los_Angeles

1. Outcome and success criteria

Add a points-funded merchandise store to the authenticated dashboard. Eligible members can browse merchandise, add variants to a cart, choose a pickup event or project-space time slot, complete an atomic checkout, and later present an order QR code. General Officers, Executive Officers, and Administrators can scan and fulfill orders. Executive Officers and Administrators manage the catalog, stock, pickup availability, orders, refunds, and point adjustments.

Members can

  • See spendable and lifetime-earned points separately.
  • Shop active products and variants without overselling stock.
  • Check out a multi-item cart with server-calculated pricing.
  • Choose an eligible event or project-space slot for pickup.
  • View order history, status, receipt, pickup details, and QR code.
  • Cancel an eligible pending order and receive an exact refund.

Staff can

  • Scan or manually enter an order code and preview the order.
  • Confirm fulfillment exactly once, with a durable audit entry.
  • See clear already-fulfilled, canceled, invalid, and wrong-pickup warnings.
  • Reschedule affected pending orders without losing capacity accounting.

Store managers can

  • Create products, images, variants, point prices, limits, and stock.
  • Designate pickup events and generate capacity-limited time slots.
  • Search orders and perform reason-required reversals or adjustments.
  • Reconcile ledger balances and inventory movement histories.
Primary invariant A completed mutation never records only half of a financial or inventory operation. Order creation, point debit, stock decrement, capacity booking, and their audit entries commit atomically in one Convex transaction—or none of them commit.

3. Current codebase findings

  • users.points is currently a mutable optional number and is incremented directly by events.checkIn.
  • Attendance records provide event history, but there is no general points ledger, purchase transaction model, or reconciliation path.
  • The overview derives its points chart only from attendance; it cannot currently represent purchases, refunds, opening balances, or adjustments.
  • The leaderboard and public profiles currently use a single points value, so they must move to lifetime-earned points while the wallet uses spendable balance.
  • The Manage Users modal exposes a points field to Administrators, but its save flow does not persist that field. This should be replaced, not repaired as a direct overwrite.
  • requireAdminAccess already means Executive Officer or Administrator; requireOfficerAccess also includes General Officers.
  • Events already have published state, location, and start/end timestamps. Pickup eligibility should be a separate store mapping so merch settings do not pollute the event workflow.
  • There is no project-space availability model. The known project-space location is EBU1-4710.
  • Convex mutations provide the transactional boundary needed for point, order, stock, and capacity consistency.
  • The app already uses Convex file storage, TanStack routes, shadcn/Radix components, Sonner, and Resend-based server email.

4. Architecture and invariants

4.1 Checkout transaction

Validate user, cart, prices, limits, and pickup
Re-check balance, stock, and capacity
Create order and immutable line snapshots
Append point and inventory ledger entries
Update summaries, book pickup, enqueue notices

All five steps execute in one Convex mutation. Concurrent checkouts conflict and retry against the same variant/account/slot documents; the retried mutation revalidates availability. This prevents negative balances, oversold variants, and overbooked slots.

4.2 Required invariants

  • All point amounts, prices, quantities, stock counts, and capacities are safe integers; prices and quantities are positive.
  • Spendable balance never becomes negative through checkout.
  • Every account summary change has exactly one corresponding ledger entry with a stable idempotency key.
  • Every stock change has an inventory movement with a resulting balance.
  • Every order state transition is validated by a centralized state machine and appends an order event.
  • Refunds and restocks reverse original entries; history is never edited or deleted.
  • Prices, products, variants, member identity, and pickup details are snapshotted on the order.
  • Client totals, roles, balance values, fulfillment state, and stock values are never trusted.
  • A checkout idempotency key returns the original order on retries and cannot create a second debit.
  • A fulfillment confirmation is idempotent; repeat scans report the existing result.

6. Backend API boundaries

Member-facing queries and mutations

  • merchCatalog.listActive: public-to-authenticated catalog projection with resolved image URLs and current availability, but no private inventory audit data.
  • points.getMyAccount and points.listMyLedger: wallet totals and paginated activity.
  • merchPickup.listAvailable: eligible event mappings and future enabled slots with remaining capacity.
  • merchOrders.checkout: accepts cart lines, expected product/variant revisions and prices, pickup choice, and a client-generated idempotency key. All totals are recomputed server-side.
  • merchOrders.listMine/getMine: minimal member-safe projections, including QR data only for the owner.
  • merchOrders.cancelMine: validates ownership, pending state, and cutoff; runs the exact reversal transaction.

Manager APIs (Executive Officer or Administrator)

  • Catalog create/update/archive, secure image upload/finalization, variant management, and inventory adjustment.
  • Pickup-event enable/disable and project-space window/slot creation and editing.
  • Paginated order search/filter/detail, cancel, reschedule, export, and action-required queues.
  • Reason-required point adjustment with an explicit correction or spendable-only mode.
  • Ledger/inventory reconciliation reports. Any repair is a new adjustment entry, never a historical rewrite.

Fulfillment APIs (General Officer or above)

  • merchFulfillment.previewByToken: authenticated token lookup returning member name, order number, items, status, and expected pickup. It performs no mutation.
  • merchFulfillment.confirm: requires token/order match plus a confirmation request ID. It accepts pending orders, records staff identity/time, and returns an idempotent already-fulfilled result on retries.
  • Manual lookup uses the same high-entropy fallback code printed below the QR; never expose sequential database IDs as redeemable codes.

7. User experience

Member store

  • Add Merch Store under Member Actions and show spendable points in the page header/cart.
  • Catalog cards show active image, point price/range, stock state, limit, and available variant choices.
  • Cart is client-side and resilient across navigation; checkout always refreshes server state. Duplicate variants merge and invalid quantities are rejected.
  • If price/revision changed, checkout returns a structured conflict; the UI shows old versus new price and requires review rather than silently charging.
  • Pickup step offers two grouped choices: manager-enabled events or available project-space time slots, displayed in Pacific time with location and cutoff.
  • Final review shows immutable line totals, overall point cost, post-purchase balance, pickup selection, and cancellation policy.
  • Success page and My Orders show receipt, pickup details, status timeline, QR, and readable fallback code.
  • QR remains hidden for canceled orders and clearly stamped as used for fulfilled orders.

Manager dashboard

  • Add Manage Merch under Executive Officers with tabs for Catalog, Orders, Pickup, Inventory, and Points.
  • Product editor supports draft preview, image upload, variants, prices, stock, limits, and availability. Activation is blocked until at least one valid active variant and image exist.
  • Inventory changes require a signed quantity and reason, show resulting stock, and warn about pending demand.
  • Pickup event selector searches approved/published future events. Project-space window builder previews generated slots before save.
  • Disabling a booked pickup option opens an impact review and requires rescheduling/canceling affected orders instead of orphaning them.
  • Orders table filters by status, product/SKU, member, pickup event/slot, overdue state, and date; detail shows all order, ledger, and audit links.
  • Balance adjustment searches a member, displays current spendable/lifetime totals, requires adjustment type and reason, previews resulting totals, and confirms explicitly.

Scanner

  • Add Merch Pickup under General Officers and above.
  • Ask for camera permission only after an explicit action; support camera selection, pause/resume, and manual code entry.
  • After decode, pause scanning and fetch a server preview. Staff verify member name and items, then press Confirm Pickup.
  • Use distinct, accessible result states for success, already fulfilled (with who/when), canceled, invalid token, unauthorized, network failure, and pickup mismatch.
  • No offline fulfillment queue: without a confirmed server response, the UI must not claim an order is fulfilled.

8. Edge-case contract

CaseRequired behavior
Double-click, timeout, reconnect, or retry at checkoutSame idempotency key returns the same order and one debit.
Two members buy the last unitOne succeeds; the other retries against updated stock and receives an out-of-stock conflict without a debit.
Two members take the last pickup slotOne succeeds; the other receives a capacity conflict and retains the cart.
Balance changes while cart is openServer rejects insufficient funds; no order, stock, or capacity records are committed.
Product price/variant changes while cart is openStructured stale-cart response prompts review; client price is never honored silently.

Scan repeated by one or multiple officersOne fulfillment event. Later confirmations show who fulfilled it and when.

9. Security, privacy, and authorization

  • Enforce every role and ownership rule in Convex functions. Sidebar visibility is only presentation.
  • Centralize requireMerchShopper, requireMerchManager, and requireMerchFulfiller helpers on top of existing authenticated-user/role helpers.
  • Generate at least 128 bits of cryptographically secure entropy for QR tokens; tokens are revocable by cancellation and are never logged in plaintext.
  • Rate-limit or throttle repeated invalid manual/token lookups where the hosting stack permits, and record security-relevant failures without storing camera frames.
  • Only order owner and authorized staff receive order data. Fulfillment preview returns the minimum necessary member identity; it does not expose PID, payment information, or unrelated profile data.
  • Validate uploaded image MIME metadata, decoded format where feasible, and size on the server; do not trust filename extensions.
  • Sanitize product text through React rendering; do not accept arbitrary HTML.
  • Record actor, reason, timestamp, and request ID for adjustments, cancellation, rescheduling, inventory changes, and fulfillment.

10. Reviewable PR sequence

Each PR is deployable behind merchStoreEnabled = false, contains its own tests, and avoids mixing broad visual refactors with accounting logic.

PR 1 — Points ledger foundation and safe migration

Goal: Establish one durable accounting path before merchandise can spend points.

Changes

  • Add pointAccounts, pointLedgerEntries, optional compatibility fields, indexes, and shared accounting helpers.
  • Implement lazy, idempotent opening-balance initialization from the current users.points. This closes the race between migration and live event check-ins.
  • Update event check-in so attendance, event reward ledger entry, account summary, lifetime total, legacy mirror, and public profile update commit together.
  • Add idempotent batch migration and read-only reconciliation commands with progress reporting and resumable cursors.
  • Switch overview/leaderboard reads: wallet uses spendable balance; leaderboard and public profile use lifetime earned. Points history comes from the ledger.

Tests

  • Opening migration rerun, legacy zero/missing/negative defense, event reward retry, duplicate attendance, and account/ledger invariants.
  • Existing event check-in and leaderboard behavior remain valid, except spending semantics are now representable.

Review boundary: accounting and migration only; no merch tables or UI.

PR 2 — Catalog, media, and inventory backend

Goal: Create auditable merchandise and stock primitives without checkout.

Changes

  • Add product, variant, and inventory-ledger schema plus manager-only mutations and member-safe catalog queries.

  • Enforce draft/active/archive lifecycle, SKU uniqueness, positive integer prices, revisioning, and archive-instead-of-delete rules.

Tests

12. Verification strategy

Automated gates per PR

  • Dashboard typecheck, Biome check, unit/integration tests, and production build.
  • Mutation tests assert both results and absence of partial writes on every rejected path.
  • Role table tests cover Member, General Officer, Executive Officer, Administrator, Sponsor, inactive, and suspended users.
  • Reconciliation tests recompute point and stock totals from ledger entries and compare summaries.
  • Accessibility tests cover labels, keyboard flow, focus recovery, status announcements, and non-color-only feedback.

Manual acceptance matrix

  • Desktop and mobile catalog/cart/checkout with event and project-space pickup.
  • Camera allowed, denied, unavailable, rear/front selection, invalid QR, and manual code.
  • Two simultaneous browsers competing for one unit and one slot.
  • Network interruption immediately after checkout/fulfillment submit followed by retry.
  • Manager archives a purchased product and edits/cancels a pickup source; historical order remains understandable.
  • Email provider unavailable; order and in-dashboard receipt remain correct.

13. Migration and rollout

  1. Deploy additive ledger schema and lazy initialization with checkout disabled.
  2. Run resumable opening-balance migration; compare account count, total legacy points, total opening entries, and reconciliation output.
  3. Verify every live point writer uses the shared helper. Repository search for direct points: patches becomes a release gate.
  4. Deploy catalog, pickup, and order foundations behind the disabled flag; configure project-space address, sample slots, products, and stock.
  5. Run a restricted officer pilot using test products and real scanner devices; inspect ledgers and retry behavior.
  6. Enable read-only storefront preview, then enable checkout. Rollback means disabling new checkout while keeping orders, QR pickup, and manager operations available.
  7. After a stable window, stop reading the legacy users.points mirror. Remove it only in a later dedicated cleanup after verifying no consumers remain.
Rollback principle Never roll back by deleting orders or ledger rows. Disable new purchasing, keep existing fulfillment/cancellation paths operational, and correct bad state with compensating entries.

14. Definition of done

  • All existing balances are represented by one opening ledger entry and reconcile exactly.
  • All future event rewards, purchases, refunds, and adjustments are ledger-backed and idempotent.
  • Leaderboard uses lifetime earned; store uses spendable balance.
  • Concurrent checkout cannot overspend, oversell, exceed limits, or overbook pickup.
  • Members can complete the full store/order/QR workflow on mobile and desktop.
  • General Officers can fulfill but cannot manage products, inventory, orders, pickup configuration, or balances.
  • Executive Officers and Administrators can operate the store and perform audited balance adjustments.
  • Event and project-space pickup choices remain understandable after source edits and can be rescheduled safely.
  • Repeat checkout, cancel, reschedule, and fulfillment requests do not duplicate side effects.
  • Notifications retry independently and never determine whether an order succeeded.
  • Reconciliation, audit history, operational runbook, and feature-flag rollback are available before launch.

15. Explicitly out of scope

  • Cash/card payments, dollar conversion, point transfers between members, or points with monetary value.
  • Shipping, delivery addresses, taxes, external fulfillment providers, or multi-location inventory.
  • Waitlists, backorders, preorders, auctions, discount codes, or dynamic pricing.
  • Post-fulfillment returns/exchanges and automatic no-show forfeiture/refunds until an organization policy is approved.
  • Offline fulfillment. Physical pickup requires a successful authenticated server confirmation.
